EditableFrames light studies: rendered example videos
=====================================================

The five clips in this folder (cafe-breakfast, ginger-ale, lemon-lime,
ai-notebook, ai-desktop) and their poster frames were rendered with
EditableFrames. Product shapes, labels, typography layouts and motion are
built in code for these studies. Every product and brand shown is an invented
concept; any resemblance to a real product is unintended. On the website they
are plain H.264 video; their editable controls live in the source projects.
