Your forecast is a strong direction—not yet a universal result.

Why it could go far

Code saves the world, cast and movements as a recipe. Better models can reuse and improve that recipe.

Game-like art fits 3D engines well. Fable’s richer characters, sets and shot direction already made a difference.

What still pushes back

Working code does not automatically create good acting, believable hands or an accurate product.

Assets, animation, sound, continuity and computer cost still matter. One draft is not proof of every future style or model.

The real dividing line

What must stay exact or changeable—and do we own the shapes and movement needed to change it?

A realistic product can be code. A fantasy action can be generated. A mask fixes the edge; it cannot teach a hand to hold something.

Future Fable versions and other providers catching up are forecasts. This exercise does not establish dates or permanent vendor leadership.

Can lighting have more than a few presets?

Yes. Light position, direction, size, colour and brightness can change continuously. Presets are starting points.

But a light only changes what the scene represents. A photographed background has baked light. A new lamp needs its glow, shadows, reflections and nearby surfaces to agree.

Our lamp trial changed the table strongly and the pastry weakly; the photographed room stayed unchanged. Mixed avatar/product relighting still needs testing.
