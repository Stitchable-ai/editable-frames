# EditableFrames brand mark

The four corners represent a frame or selection boundary. The diagonal pencil, with a separated cap and nib, represents editing the contents. The original corner geometry is preserved.

- `editableframes-mark.svg`: transparent dark mark for light backgrounds.
- `editableframes-mark-light.svg`: transparent lime mark for dark backgrounds.
- `editableframes-icon.svg`: blue application tile with the lime mark.

SVGs are resolution independent. Keep the gaps between the pencil cap, body and nib; they distinguish the tool at small sizes. Keep at least one quarter of the mark width as clear space in standalone applications. Original artwork is covered by the repository MIT license.

- `editableframes-wordmark.svg` and `editableframes-wordmark-light.svg`: name-and-icon lockups with a small ™ claim beside the name. Keep the standalone icon free of trademark symbols.

Use EditableFrames™ in prominent branded names; ordinary prose, package names, URLs and code identifiers remain EditableFrames. ™ is a trademark claim, not a statement of registration. Do not use ® without the applicable registration.
